Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026 · BYOS Computer Systems Pvt. Ltd., 42, 3rd Cross Street, RS Puram, Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu 641002 · GSTIN: 33AABCB1234F1Z5

1. What we collect

When you order or request a quote, we collect your name, phone, email, delivery address and — for B2B orders — your GSTIN. Payment details are processed by Razorpay and are never stored on our servers. This website stores your cart and build locally in your browser (localStorage) so it works offline; no tracking cookies are used for advertising.

2. How we use it

  • To assemble, test and deliver your PC or component order.
  • To issue GST tax invoices and provide warranty support.
  • To send order status updates via SMS, WhatsApp or email.

We never sell or rent your personal data. Order data may be synced to our Google Sheets backend solely for fulfilment and accounting.

3. Your rights

You may request access, correction or deletion of your personal data at any time by writing to [email protected] or calling +91 422 400 1234. We respond within 7 working days as per the Information Technology Act, 2000 and applicable Tamil Nadu regulations.

4. Data retention

Tax invoices and GST records are retained for 8 years as required under the CGST Act. Other order data is retained for 3 years for warranty purposes, then anonymised.

Terms & Conditions

Last updated: 24 November 2026 · BYOS Computer Systems Pvt. Ltd., Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu.

1. Orders & pricing

All prices are in INR and include GST (CGST 9% + SGST 9% within Tamil Nadu; IGST 18% inter-state) unless marked exclusive. Custom build quotations are valid for 15 days. An order is confirmed only after payment or store-pickup confirmation.

2. Assembly & delivery

Custom PCs are assembled and stress-tested for 48 hours before dispatch. Standard build time is 3–5 working days. Delivery is free within Coimbatore city limits; insured courier elsewhere is charged at actuals. Risk of loss transfers on delivery acknowledgement.

3. Warranty

Components carry the respective manufacturer's warranty (AMD, Intel, NVIDIA, etc.). BYOS provides a 3-year service warranty on assembly and cabling. Physical damage, liquid damage, unauthorised opening and overclocking beyond manufacturer limits void warranty.

4. Returns & DOA

Dead-on-arrival components are replaced within 7 days of delivery on presentation of the GST invoice and original packaging. Sealed accessories may be returned within 7 days if unused. Custom-assembled PCs are covered under warranty, not return.

5. Liability

Our liability is limited to the invoice value of the goods supplied. We are not liable for data loss — customers are advised to maintain backups before submitting systems for service.
